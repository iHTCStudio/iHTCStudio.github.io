**May “I examine myself three times a day” become a strength in ordinary hours.**

iZengZi (爱曾子 / 愛曾子) is an **offline workshop** for a **closed Zengzi line** — not the complete Analects, and not the complete Mencius. **No account required.** Five related public-domain sources ship as **66** chapters: Zengzi passages from the *Analects* and *Mencius*, the *Great Learning*, the *Classic of Filial Piety*, and the Zengzi chapters of the *Da Dai Liji*, with vernacular and learning English. Keep a **three-question journal**. Walk the **eight steps**. Meet **Daily Reflection** on the Home Screen. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

Optional HealthKit mood write is **off by default** and is **not** a mental-health product.

**At a glance:** 66 chapters · 255 sentences · 24 tales · 8 concepts · three-question journal · eight steps · Daily Reflection · `izengzi://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813770441) and open **iZengZi**.
2. The welcome guide leads with Daily Reflection, the journal, and the eight steps. Replay: **Settings → Help**.
3. First launch **never** asks for Health access.

### 2. Read today’s reflection

1. Open **Home**. **Daily Reflection** shows today’s chapter.
2. Random explores only — it does **not** replace today.

### 3. Journal & eight steps

1. Open the journal (`izengzi://reflect`): loyalty, trust, practice — a private note on this device.
2. If you later want a Health mood mark, enable it in **journal settings**, not at first launch.
3. Open the eight steps (`izengzi://eight`) and jump back to the matching chapter.

### 4. Recite & Cultivation Hall

1. Recite with line highlighting and a playback-content matrix.
2. **Cultivation Hall**: fade, fill-blank, quiz, and Keyword Hunt.

### 5. Privacy & sync

1. Core use is **offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud** syncs favorites, lists, notes, history, streaks, and journal entries through **your** iCloud.

## Why you’ll want to download

- **A closed set** — Zengzi’s line only, so the desk cannot grow into a title store.
- **Examine, then write** — the journal is a private cultivation record, not a scoreboard.
- **One desk** — Daily Reflection, recite, study, and widgets share the same 66 chapters.
- **Privacy by design** — no account, no ads, no analytics SDK.

- 66 chapters · 255 sentences · 20 famous lines · 24 tales · 8 concepts · **full chapter appreciations** for long-form reading

### Unique desk
