<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Cultivation Hall, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

It is a **closed Zengzi-line desk**: related chapters from the Analects and Mencius, the *Great Learning*, the *Classic of Filial Piety*, and the Zengzi chapters of the *Da Dai Liji*, plus a three-question journal and the eight steps — not the complete Four Books.

### What texts are included?

66 chapters, 255 sentences, 20 famous lines, 24 tales, 8 concepts, from five related public-domain sources. Learning English draws on James Legge public-domain renderings.

### Do you read my Health data?

**No.** If you opt in, the app **writes** a mood mark to your Health app. iHTC Studio does not receive it. First launch never asks for Health access.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

The listing is being prepared. Bookmark this support page; the [App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813770441) will open once Apple publishes the app.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。语料与学习辅助均内置。安装后浏览、对照品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、修身学堂、小组件与每日通知均可**离线**使用。可选例外：**StoreKit 打赏**（Apple）、分享到你选择的其他 App，以及可选 iCloud。

### 和普通电子书有什么不同？

这是**曾子一线封闭书桌**：论语 / 孟子相关章、《大学》、《孝经》、大戴礼记曾子，以及三省日记与八条目——不是四书全集。

### 收录了哪些内容？

66 篇、255 句、20 名句、24 则典故、8 个概念，来自五部相关公版文献。学习用英译参考 Legge 公版译本。

### 你们会读取我的健康数据吗？

**不会。** 若你选择开启，应用只向你的健康 App **写入**心境标记。iHTC Studio 收不到。首次启动不会索要健康权限。

### iCloud 同步覆盖什么？

可选同步 **收藏**、**清单**、**笔记**、**阅读历史**、**学习进度**与**打卡**，经由你的私人 iCloud KVS。

### 你们会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见[隐私政策](privacy)：iHTC Studio 为 Data Not Collected；无广告／分析 SDK；无账号。
