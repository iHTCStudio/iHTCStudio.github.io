<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The Five Classics library ships on device. Reading, recite, study, widgets, and local notifications work offline after install.

### How is this different from a simple classics reader?

iWuJing is a **study desk**, not a static ebook: bilingual reading, gloss, variants, recitation, Classics Study, Daily Classic, widgets, Spotlight, and deep links.

### What texts are included?

**819 passages** across *Shijing*, *Shujing*, *Liji*, *Yijing*, and *Chunqiu with Zuo Tradition*.

### Does the app do divination for Zhouyi?

**No.** Zhouyi is presented strictly for classical study. There is no fortune-telling, casting, or prediction feature.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy).

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6797013704). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。五经语料随应用内置，阅读、诵读、学堂、小组件和本地通知在安装后即可离线使用。

### 和普通经典阅读器有什么不同？

爱五经是**经学工作台**，不是静态电子书：中英对照、训诂、异文、诵读、经学学堂、每日一经、小组件、Spotlight 和深链都可配合使用。

### 收录了哪些内容？

共 **819 篇**，覆盖《诗经》《尚书》《礼记》《周易》《春秋左传》。

### 会不会做《周易》占卜？

**不会。** 《周易》只用于经学研读，不提供算卦、占卜、抽签、运势或预测功能。

### iCloud 同步包含什么？

收藏、经单、历史、进度、打卡与笔记。

### 你们收集我的数据吗？

**不收集。** 详见[隐私政策](../privacy)。
