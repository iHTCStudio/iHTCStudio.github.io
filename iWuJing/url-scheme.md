<section lang="en" markdown="1">

iWuJing supports **`iwujing://`** shortcut links.

## Common links

- `iwujing://daily` — today’s Daily Classic
- `iwujing://random` — random passage
- `iwujing://passage?id=1001` — passage detail
- `iwujing://recite?id=1001` — recite a passage
- `iwujing://study?id=1001` — study a passage
- `iwujing://favorites` — favorites
- `iwujing://search?q=关雎` — search
- `iwujing://collection?id=shijing` — a classic collection
- `iwujing://theme?id=wujing` — a theme
- `iwujing://notes` — notes
- `iwujing://settings` — settings

</section>
