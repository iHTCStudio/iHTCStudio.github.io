**Learning never stops — read Xunzi into daily life.**

iXunZi (爱荀子 / 愛荀子) is an **offline Xunzi studio** — not another plain text dump. **No account required.** The *Xunzi* library ships on your device with **original / vernacular / English**. Start from **Daily Xun**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Encouraging Learning Academy**. Play **Xun Hunt**. Export **荀 cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Optional **Ask Xunzi** uses Apple on-device Intelligence when available. Originals are public-domain study texts; notes are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 32 chapters · 1442 segments · 100 famous lines · concept lexicon (24) · idiom lexicon (18) · 6 Mencius contrasts · original / vernacular / English · recite · Encouraging Learning Academy · Xun Hunt · Daily Xun · 荀 cards · widgets · Control Center · `ixunzi://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6475755747) and open **iXunZi**.
2. Complete the welcome guide (daily reading → close reading → recite → study → system features → privacy).
3. Replay anytime: **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`ixunzi://feature-guide`).
4. Usage / Widget / Shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s passage on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Daily Xun** hero shows today’s passage; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Daily Xun notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Encouraging Learning Academy, Xun Hunt, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse the full *Xunzi* corpus, famous lines, and specialty shelves.
2. Search titles and lines (Simplified / Traditional friendly). On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star passages into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **荀单 lists**.

### 4. Close reading & share cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle layers independently where available.
2. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, font scale, and segmented pages for longer passages.
2. Optional **Ask Xunzi**: `ixunzi://ask` — on-device Apple Intelligence when available; falls back to bundled notes.
3. Open **荀 cards** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Encouraging Learning Academy

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Encouraging Learning Academy** (`ixunzi://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, and quiz, with mastery tracking.
3. **Xun Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add Daily / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress widgets (and Almanac where available).
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `ixunzi://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), study, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.
