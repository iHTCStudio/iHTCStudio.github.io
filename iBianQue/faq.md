<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Que Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

It is a **Nan Jing inquiry desk**: question-and-answer Difficulties, Four Examinations, a 2D meridian atlas, Que Study, and Daily Art — cultural study, not a clinic and not a static ebook.

### What texts are included?

Bian Que’s *Shiji* memoir, all 81 Difficulties of the *Nan Jing*, selected Inner Canon chapters, 24 solar-term cards, 51 acupoint study cards, 10 lore pieces, 24 concepts. Originals are public-domain. Vernacular and learning English are study aids.

### Is this a medical app?

**No.** Scene cards, the atlas, and the tongue sketch are cultural study of historical texts. They cannot replace professional medical advice.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

The listing is being prepared. Bookmark this support page; the [App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813769658) will open once Apple publishes the app.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

这是**难经问难书桌**：问答体八十一难、四诊馆、二维经络图谱、鹊学堂与今日一术——文化学习，不是诊所，也不是静态电子书。

**不是。** 情景卡、图谱与舌象示意是历史文本的文化学习，不能替代专业医疗意见。
