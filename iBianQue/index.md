**From Daily Art — rediscover Bian Que in a single glance and a single question.**

iBianQue (爱扁鹊 / 愛扁鵲) is an **offline cultural workshop** for Bian Que and the *Nan Jing* — not a clinic, and not another quote wall. **No account required.** The library ships on your device: Bian Que’s *Shiji* memoir, all **81 Difficulties**, selected Inner Canon chapters, and **24** solar-term cards, with **vernacular** and **learning English**. Read the inquiry form side by side. Open the **Four Examinations** gallery and the **2D meridian atlas** as cultural diagrams. Practice in **Que Study**. Keep **Daily Art** on the Home Screen. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

This is **cultural study of historical texts**. It **cannot replace professional medical advice**.

**At a glance:** 141 passages · 511 sentences · 81 Difficulties · Four Examinations · 51 acupoint study cards · 24 solar terms · 10 lore pieces · Daily Art · Que Study · `ibianque://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813769658) and open **iBianQue**.
2. The welcome guide leads with inquiry, Four Examinations, and the atlas — not a generic reader tour. Replay anytime: **Settings → Help**.
3. Usage / Widget / Shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s art on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab. **Daily Art** shows today’s passage; tap to open detail.
2. Optional: enable **local** Daily Art notifications in Settings.
3. Random passage explores only — it does **not** replace today.

### 3. Inquiry, atlas & Four Examinations

1. Open a *Nan Jing* Difficulty and read it as question then answer.
2. Open the **atlas** (`ibianque://atlas`): tap a point, filter by meridian. Cards use international codes (LU / LI / ST…). Schematic learning only.
3. **Four Examinations** and the tongue color sketch stay on device. They are cultural scenes, not a diagnosis.

### 4. Close reading, recite & Que Study

1. Switch bilingual / Chinese only / English only; one gloss line under the original.
2. Recite with line highlighting, a playback-content matrix, and optional Live Activity.
3. **Que Study**: fade, fill-blank, sense check, quiz, plus Point Hunt.

### 5. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), study, widgets, and local notifications work **offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud** mirrors favorites, lists, notes, history, and streaks through **your** iCloud.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall.
