**Knowing and acting as one.**

iXin (爱王阳明 / 愛王陽明) is an **offline Yangming studio** — not another *Chuanxilu* dump. **No account required.** All **102** passages ship on your device: *Instructions for Practical Living*, *Inquiry on the Great Learning*, *Instructions to Students at Longchang*, *Essay on Burying Travelers*, plus poems, annals, and concept cards. Every work carries **vernacular Chinese** and **English** in the spirit of Henke (1916). Unique **Knowing-Acting journal** and **Three Questions of Liangzhi** turn 知行合一 into a daily practice. Start from **Daily Insight**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Heart School**. Export **insight cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Originals are public-domain; notes are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 102 passages · 8 authors · 100 famous lines · bilingual · Knowing-Acting journal · Liangzhi prompts · graph / timeline / map / people · recite · Heart School · Heart Hunt · Daily Insight · insight cards · widgets · Control Center · `ixin://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1180009069) and open **iXin**.
2. Complete the welcome guide: Daily Insight → recitation → close reading → Heart School → journal → Liangzhi → gestures → privacy.
3. Replay anytime in **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`ixin://feature-guide`).
4. Usage, widget, and shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s insight on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Daily Insight** hero shows today’s passage; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Daily Insight notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Heart School, Heart Hunt, journal, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse *Chuanxilu*, *Inquiry on the Great Learning*, Longchang instructions, *Burial Essay*, poems / annals / concepts, people, and **100 famous lines**.
2. Search titles, source text, and famous lines. On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star passages into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **lists** (心单).

### 4. Close reading, journal & insight cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle vernacular, English, and pinyin independently.
2. Open the **Knowing-Acting journal** (`ixin://journal`) and **Three Questions of Liangzhi** (`ixin://liangzhi`). Concept graph, timeline, map, and people: `ixin://graph` / `timeline` / `map` / `people`.
3. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, centering, font scale, and segmented pages.
4. Open **Insight Card** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Heart School

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Heart School** (`ixin://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, and quiz, with mastery tracking.
3. **Heart Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add **Daily Insight / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress / Journal** widgets.
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `ixin://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), Heart School, journal, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, notes, **and journal entries** through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.
