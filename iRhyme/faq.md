<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library, vernacular, English, and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Rhyme Academy, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iRhyme is a **sound-rhyme studio**: **parallelism visualization**, **same-rhyme compare**, a **93-term glossary**, karaoke-style **recite**, **Rhyme Academy**, **Rhyme Hunt**, **Today’s Rhyme**, rhyme cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of couplets.

### What texts are included?

**312** passages:

- *Shenglü Qimeng* (Sound and Rhyme Primer)
- *Li Weng Dui Yun* across **thirty rhymes**
- *Qian Jia Shi* (Poems of a Thousand Masters)
- *You Xue Qiong Lin* (Jade Forest for Beginners)

**127** authors · **120** famous lines · **93** glossary entries.

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, and notes are curated study aids, not academic editions.

### What is parallelism visualization?

Couplet lines are mapped so you can *see* 对仗 — antithesis, part of speech, and rhyme — instead of only reading the characters. **Same-rhyme compare** (`irhyme://compare`) lines up *Shenglü* and *Li Weng* in one rhyme class (e.g. `dong`). A **glossary** (`irhyme://glossary`) explains 93 technical terms.


### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, and **quiz**, with mastery tracking. **Rhyme Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game. Pairing practice lives at `irhyme://pair`.

### What is Today’s Rhyme?

A daily passage on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists (韵单)**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](../privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1439667243). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.
