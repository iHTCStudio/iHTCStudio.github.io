**The Liezi still rides light — from Yugong back to the thought that holds it.**

iLiezi (爱列子 / 愛列子) is an **offline workshop** for one classical text: the received *Liezi*. **No account required.** All **135** story-units across eight parts ship on your device, **2,544** sentences aligned across original, vernacular, and learning English. Open **Yugong**, **riding the wind**, and **the man of Qi** from the fable hall. Walk the **xu / wind / ming** graph. Keep **Daily Liezi** on the Home Screen. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

The Yang Zhu book is kept in full with a scholarly note: intellectual history, not a lifestyle slogan.

**At a glance:** 135 units · 2,544 sentences · 16 fables · 8 concepts · Daily Liezi · Liezi Study · `iliezi://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started

### 1. First launch

1. When the App Store listing is live, install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813770086) and open **iLiezi**.
2. The welcome guide leads with fables and xu — not a generic bilingual tour. Replay: **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s unit

1. Open **Home**. **Daily Liezi** shows today’s story-unit.
2. Random explores only — it does **not** replace today.

### 3. Fable hall & the three pillars

1. Open the fable hall (`iliezi://fables`) and jump from story to source sentence.
2. Open the xu / wind / ming graph (`iliezi://concepts`).
3. Representative units open directly — no catalog detour required.

### 4. Recite & Liezi Study

1. Recite with line highlighting and a playback-content matrix.
2. **Liezi Study**: fade, fill-blank, quiz, fable match, and Hunt Liezi.

### 5. Privacy & sync

1. Core use is **offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud** syncs favorites, lists, notes, history, and streaks through **your** iCloud.

## Why you’ll want to download

- **One book, read deep** — eight parts, not a quote list.
- **Fables that return to the line** — Yugong, wind, and the man of Qi walk back to the source.
- **One desk** — Daily Liezi, recite, study, and widgets share the same units.
- **Privacy by design** — no account, no ads, no analytics SDK.
