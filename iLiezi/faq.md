<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Liezi Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

It is a **complete Liezi desk**: 135 story-units, a fable hall (Yugong, riding the wind, the man of Qi), and the xu / wind / ming graph — one book, not a quote list.

### What texts are included?

All 135 story-units across eight parts, 2,544 aligned sentences, 19 famous lines, 16 fables, 8 concepts. Originals are public-domain. Study English is aligned with the public-domain Giles 1912 selection — not Graham 1960.

### Can I open Yugong without browsing the whole catalog?

Yes. The fable hall and Home shortcuts open representative units such as Yugong, riding the wind, and the man of Qi directly.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

The listing is being prepared. Bookmark this support page; the [App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813770086) will open once Apple publishes the app.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。语料与学习辅助均内置。安装后浏览、对照品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、列学学堂、小组件与每日通知均可**离线**使用。可选例外：**StoreKit 打赏**（Apple）、分享到你选择的其他 App，以及可选 iCloud。

### 和普通电子书有什么不同？

这是**完整列子书桌**：135 则、寓言馆（愚公、御风、杞人）与贵虚 / 风 / 命三柱——只做一部书，不是语录墙。

### 收录了哪些内容？

八卷 135 则、2544 句对齐、19 名句、16 则寓言、8 条概念。原文为公版。学习用英译参照 Giles 1912 公版选译，不用 Graham 1960。

### 不翻完整目录也能打开愚公吗？

可以。寓言馆与首页快捷可直达愚公、御风、杞人等代表则。

### iCloud 同步覆盖什么？

可选同步 **收藏**、**清单**、**笔记**、**阅读历史**、**学习进度**与**打卡**，经由你的私人 iCloud KVS。

### 你们会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见[隐私政策](privacy)：iHTC Studio 为 Data Not Collected；无广告／分析 SDK；无账号。

### App Store Connect 的隐私政策 URL 在哪里？

打开本站[隐私政策](privacy)页，从浏览器地址栏复制当前网址。

### 系统要求？

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 14.0+**。

### 如何联系支持？

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — 通常 48 小时内回复。
