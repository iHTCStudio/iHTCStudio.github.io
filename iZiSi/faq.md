### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Zisi Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

It is a **Zisi-line desk**: Zhu Xi’s 33 *Zhongyong* chapters, *Biaoji* / *Fangji* / *Ziyi*, Guodian Five Conducts and *Xing Zi Ming Chu*, plus five steps and Watchfulness studio — not the Four Books bundle.

### What texts are included?

About 115 chapters including 33 *Zhongyong* chapters, related *Liji* pieces, Guodian slips, anecdotes, famous lines, and concepts. Guodian material is bundled public-domain historical text for academic parallel reading.

### Is the Five Conducts gallery the popular five-elements fortune system?

**No.** It is the Guodian slip text: humaneness, rightness, ritual, wisdom, sageliness, read beside *Zhongyong*. Not metal–wood–water–fire–earth fortune-telling.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

The listing is being prepared. Bookmark this support page; the [App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813770592) will open once Apple publishes the app.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 应用是完全离线的吗？

核心功能是。语料与学习辅助均内置。安装后浏览、对照品读、诵读（系统 TTS）、子思学堂、小组件与每日通知均可**离线**使用。可选例外：**StoreKit 打赏**（Apple）、分享到你选择的其他 App，以及可选 iCloud。

### 和普通电子书有什么不同？

这是**子思一线书桌**：朱熹《中庸》33 章、表记 / 坊记 / 缁衣、郭店《五行》与《性自命出》，以及为学五步与慎独工坊——不是四书全集。

### 收录了哪些内容？

约 115 篇，含《中庸》33 章、相关礼记篇、郭店简、轶事、名句与概念。郭店材料为随包离线的公版历史文本，供学术对读。

### 五行馆是民间金木水火土算命吗？

**不是。** 那是郭店简文：仁、义、礼、智、圣，与《中庸》对读。不是金木水火土算命。

### iCloud 同步覆盖什么？

可选同步 **收藏**、**清单**、**笔记**、**阅读历史**、**学习进度**与**打卡**，经由你的私人 iCloud KVS。

### 你们会收集我的数据吗？

**不会。** 见[隐私政策](privacy)：iHTC Studio 为 Data Not Collected；无广告／分析 SDK；无账号。

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**，Mac **macOS 14.0+**。
