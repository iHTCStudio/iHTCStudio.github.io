**From Today’s Thought — between the Mean and watchfulness.**

iZiSi (爱子思 / 愛子思) is an **offline Zisi-line workshop** — not the Four Books bundle. **No account required.** Zhu Xi’s **33** *Zhongyong* chapters ship with *Biaoji*, *Fangji*, *Ziyi*, the Guodian **Five Conducts**, and *Xing Zi Ming Chu*, plus vernacular and learning English. Walk the **five steps of learning**. Keep watch in the **Watchfulness studio**. Read the slips beside the Mean. Meet **Today’s Thought** on the Home Screen. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

Watchfulness is an **inner reading ritual**, not a meditation tracker and not a camera on other people. The Five Conducts gallery is **academic parallel reading**, not fortune-telling.

**At a glance:** 33 *Zhongyong* chapters · Guodian slips · five steps · Watchfulness studio · lineage map · Today’s Thought · `izisi://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started

### 1. First launch

1. When the App Store listing is live, install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813770592) and open **iZiSi**.
2. The welcome guide leads with Today’s Thought, Five Conducts, and Watchfulness. Replay: **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s thought

1. Open **Home**. **Today’s Thought** shows today’s chapter.
2. Random explores only — it does **not** replace today.
3. The Home quick grid (`izisi://home-quick`) can hold up to twelve shortcuts you arrange.

### 3. Five steps, Watchfulness & Five Conducts

1. Open the five steps (`izisi://five-steps`): broad study → inquiry → careful thought → discernment → practice. You mark a step when walked.
2. Open Watchfulness (`izisi://shendu`): a night line, a stillness timer, notes on the unissued and the issued.
3. Open the Five Conducts gallery (`izisi://wuxing`) and jump to the matching *Zhongyong* chapter.

### 4. Recite & Zisi Study

1. Recite with line highlighting and a playback-content matrix. Live Activity uses the vermilion seal **Si**.
2. **Zisi Study**: fade, fill-blank, quiz, virtue matching, and Find the Mean.

### 5. Privacy & sync

1. Core use is **offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud** syncs favorites, lists, notes, history, streaks, Watchfulness reflections, and five-step marks through **your** iCloud.

## Why you’ll want to download

- **Zisi’s line only** — the Mean, sincerity, and watchfulness, not a Four Books stall.
- **Slips beside the book** — Guodian Five Conducts as academic parallel, not a relic shop.
- **One desk** — Today’s Thought, five steps, Watchfulness, and widgets share the same library.
- **Privacy by design** — no account, no ads, no analytics SDK.
