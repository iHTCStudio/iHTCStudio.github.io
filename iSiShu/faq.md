<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The Four Books library ships on device. Reading, recite, study, widgets, and local notifications work offline after install.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iSiShu is a **study space** with trilingual reading, recitation, focused study, Daily Saying, widgets, Spotlight, and deep links.

### What texts are included?

**1257 passages** from *Daxue*, *Zhongyong*, *Lunyu*, and *Mengzi*.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy).

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6797013698). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.
