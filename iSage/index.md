**One saying. A way to live.**

iSage (爱增广贤文 / 愛增廣賢文) is an **offline companion for *Zengguang Xianwen*** — the *Augmented Wisdom of the Sages* — not another plain-text dump. **No account required.** All **1084** sayings ship on your device: the **classic recension** (348), plus Zhou Xitao’s ***New Augmented Wisdom***, **Part I** (433) and **Part II** (303). Every saying carries **vernacular Chinese**, **English**, a curated **sage reading**, and **2–4 modern life scenes**, so a proverb can settle into how you treat people, keep house, and choose. Start from contextual **Today’s Wisdom**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Sage Academy**. Export **sage cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Originals are public-domain; notes and scenes are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 1084 sayings · three recensions · 12 themes · 120 famous lines · bilingual + interpretation + scenes · recite · Sage Academy · Sage Hunt · Today’s Wisdom · sage cards · widgets · Control Center · `isage://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1231833729) and open **iSage**.
2. Complete the welcome guide: Today’s Wisdom → recitation → close reading → Sage Academy → life scenes → gestures → privacy.
3. Replay anytime in **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`isage://feature-guide`).
4. Usage, widget, and shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s wisdom on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Today’s Wisdom** hero shows today’s saying; tap to open detail.
3. Optional: enable local **Today’s Wisdom notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Sage Academy, Sage Hunt, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse by **classic recension**, **Zhou Part I / Part II**, **12 themes** (conduct, cultivation, learning, friendship, household, wealth, virtue, fortune, speech, loyalty, diligence, the world), and **120 famous lines**.
2. Search titles, source text, and famous lines. On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star sayings into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **lists** (贤单).

### 4. Close reading, scenes & sage cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle vernacular, English, and pinyin independently.
2. Open the **sage reading** and **life scenes** on the same page — each saying maps onto 2–4 everyday situations.
3. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, centering, font scale, and segmented pages for longer passages.
4. Open **Sage Card** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Sage Academy

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Sage Academy** (`isage://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, quiz, **couplet pairing**, and **scene choice**, with mastery tracking.
3. **Sage Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-saying game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add **Today’s Wisdom / Scene / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress** widgets.
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `isage://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), Sage Academy, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.
