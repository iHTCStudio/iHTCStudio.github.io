<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. All **1084** sayings, vernacular, English, sage readings, and life scenes ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Sage Academy, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

iSage is a **wisdom studio**: bilingual close reading, curated **sage interpretations**, **life scenes** that map a proverb onto daily choices, karaoke-style **recite**, **Sage Academy** (fade / fill-blank / self-check / quiz / couplet pairing / scene choice), **Sage Hunt**, contextual **Today’s Wisdom**, sage cards, widgets, optional Live Activity, Siri & Shortcuts — not a static dump of text.

### What texts are included?

**1084** sayings from *Zengguang Xianwen* (*Augmented Wisdom of the Sages*):

- Classic recension — **348**
- Zhou Xitao, *New Augmented Wisdom*, Part I — **433**
- Zhou Xitao, *New Augmented Wisdom*, Part II — **303**

Originals are public-domain. Vernacular, English, interpretations, and scenes are curated study aids, not academic editions.

### What are life scenes?

Each saying includes **2–4** pre-written modern situations — how you treat people, keep house, speak, or choose — so a couplet can be used the same day. They are bundled offline, not generated live.

### What study modes are available?

**Fade**, **fill-blank**, **self-check**, **quiz**, **couplet pairing**, and **scene choice**, with mastery tracking. **Sage Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-saying game.

### What is Today’s Wisdom?

A daily saying on Home, optionally **contextual**, with optional **local** reminders. Widgets can keep it on the Home Screen, Lock Screen, and StandBy.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](../privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1231833729). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.
