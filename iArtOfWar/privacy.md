**Last updated:** July 29, 2026

iHTC Studio ("we", "us" or "our") built **iArtOfWar** (also known as "爱兵法" / "愛兵法", Bundle ID `com.iHTCboy.iArtOfWar`). This Privacy Policy explains what data is (and is not) handled when you use the app. **Apple App Review** and users may rely on this page as the public privacy URL.

## Summary (Apple Privacy Nutrition Label alignment)

| Topic | Our practice |
|-------|----------------|
| Account | **Not required** |
| Data collection by iHTC Studio | **Data Not Collected** |
| Network | Library, reading, recite, study, widgets, and daily notifications work **offline** after install |
| Analytics / ads / tracking | **None** |
| Optional Apple services | **iCloud** (your private KVS), **Photo Library (Add Only)** for share cards, **StoreKit** tipping, **local notifications**, optional **Live Activities** |

## Data Collection

We do **not** collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising or analytics. Your reading history, favorites, lists, and preferences stay on your device (and optionally in **your** iCloud). They are never uploaded to iHTC Studio servers.

## How the App Works

- **Bundled library** — Passage JSON and indexes ship inside the app / widget bundle.
- **On-device speech** — Recitation uses Apple’s system **AVSpeechSynthesizer**.
- **Widgets & App Group** — Shared via App Group `group.com.iHTCboy.iArtOfWar` on your device.
- **Daily notifications** — Scheduled locally; tapping opens a deep link to a passage ID on device.
- **Share cards** — Rendered on device; saving uses **Photo Library add-only**.
- **Optional tip** — Consumable **StoreKit** purchase processed by Apple.

## Data Stored on Your Device

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Passage database | Offline reading & study |
| Favorites & strategy lists | Starred works and user lists |
| Reading history | Continue reading |
| Study progress & mastery | Classroom state |
| Streak / check-in | Learning streaks |
| Appearance & reading prefs | Theme, language, TTS, tabs, widgets |
| Daily stratagem cache & notification prefs | Today’s ID and schedule |
| Onboarding state | Welcome guide |

## iCloud Sync (Optional)

If enabled, the app mirrors favorites, strategy lists, reading history, study progress, and streaks through Apple’s **`NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore`** tied to **your Apple ID**. Sync traffic goes to **Apple iCloud**, not to iHTC Studio.

## Permissions

| Permission | When | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| Notifications | You enable Daily Stratagem reminders | Local scheduling |
| Photo Library (Add Only) | You save a share card | Write the rendered image |
| iCloud | You enable sync | Sync favorites / lists / history / progress / streaks |
| Live Activities (iOS) | You enable Reading Live Activity | Lock Screen / Dynamic Island |
| Background audio | You enable background recitation | Continue TTS in background |

We do **not** request Contacts, precise Location, Camera, Microphone (beyond system TTS playback), or Tracking.
