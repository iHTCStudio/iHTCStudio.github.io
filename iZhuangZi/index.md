**One passage of Zhuangzi. Untroubled ease.**

iZhuangZi (爱庄子 / 愛莊子) is an **offline Zhuangzi studio** — not another *Xiaoyaoyou* dump. **No account required.** All **33** chapters ship on your device: Inner **7**, Outer **15**, Miscellaneous **11**, with **701** paragraphs of **vernacular Chinese** and **James Legge (1891)** English. **Twenty parables** open the book as stories. Optional **Ask Zhuangzi** uses Apple on-device Intelligence when available. Start from **Daily Zhuangzi**. **Recite** with paragraph highlighting. Practice in **Dao Study**. Export **逍遥 cards**. Add **widgets**, optional **Reading Live Activity**, **Siri & Shortcuts**, and optional **iCloud**. Originals are public-domain; notes are study aids. **No ads. No tracking. Data Not Collected.**

**At a glance:** 33 chapters · 701 paragraphs · 20 parables · 90 famous lines · bilingual · Ask Zhuangzi · recite · Dao Study · Zhuang Hunt · Daily Zhuangzi · 逍遥 cards · widgets · Control Center · `izhuangzi://` · EN / 简 / 繁.

## Getting Started (step by step)

### 1. First launch

1. Install from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1439667592) and open **iZhuangZi**.
2. Complete the welcome guide: Daily Zhuangzi → recitation → close reading → Dao Study → parables → Ask Zhuangzi → gestures → privacy.
3. Replay anytime in **Settings → Help → Feature Guide** (`izhuangzi://feature-guide`).
4. Usage, widget, and shortcuts guides live under **Settings → Help**.

### 2. Read today’s Zhuangzi on Home

1. Open the **Home** tab.
2. The **Daily Zhuangzi** hero shows today’s chapter; tap to open detail. Use **refresh** to swap another chapter for today.
3. Optional: enable local **Daily Zhuangzi notifications** in Settings.
4. Jump from Home into recite, Dao Study, Zhuang Hunt, parables, favorites, and search.

### 3. Browse & search

1. Browse **Inner / Outer / Miscellaneous** chapters, **20 parables**, concepts, and **90 famous lines**.
2. Search titles, source text, and famous lines. On iOS, optional **handwriting search** recognizes characters on device.
3. Star chapters into **Favorites** and gather them into personal **lists** (庄单).

### 4. Close reading, parables & 逍遥 cards

1. Switch **bilingual / Chinese only / English only**; toggle vernacular, English, and pinyin independently.
2. Open **parables** (`izhuangzi://allegories`) and **concepts** (`izhuangzi://concepts`). Optional **Ask Zhuangzi**: `izhuangzi://ask`.
3. Try immersive reading, vertical layout, centering, font scale, and segmented pages for longer chapters.
4. Open **逍遥 Card** → pick style / ratio → save to Photos (add-only) or system share.

### 5. Recite & Dao Study

1. Open the recitation workbench for highlighted TTS, adjustable voice and pace, optional vernacular / English captions, continuous play, and background audio.
2. **Dao Study** (`izhuangzi://study`): fade, fill-blank, self-check, and quiz, with mastery tracking.
3. **Zhuang Hunt** on Home is a separate find-the-passage game.

### 6. Widgets, Siri & system features

1. Add **Daily Zhuangzi / Famous Line / Favorites / Study Progress** widgets.
2. Use Control Center, Siri, Spotlight, and `izhuangzi://` deep links.
3. Optional Reading Live Activity stays available on supported iPhone devices.

### 7. Privacy & sync

1. Library, reading, recite (system TTS), Dao Study, widgets, and local notifications work **fully offline** after install. **Ask Zhuangzi** stays on device when Apple Intelligence is available.
2. Optional **iCloud Sync** mirrors favorites, lists, history, progress, streaks, and notes through **your private iCloud**.
3. Tip the developer (optional StoreKit) never unlocks a paywall and never creates an account.
