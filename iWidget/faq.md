### Is iWidget a calendar app?

No. iWidget is a **DIY widget studio**. Calendar, weather, photos, and launchers are **blocks** you place on a grid. **Today** is a convenient dashboard; Home Screen widgets use **saved designs** from **My Widgets**.

### How is iWidget different from iCalendar?

Different product, interface, and widget style. iWidget focuses on **layout**, **wallpaper tint**, **transparent slots**, and **tap shield**. Calendar tables share lineage for lunar accuracy, but the apps are separate on the App Store.

### How many designs and blocks are there?

**72** design sets and **70** addable block types. Browse the library by set or by size. Extra-large sizes appear on supported devices. The same set can be saved as multiple copies for different wallpapers.

### Why is My Widgets empty after the first launch?

That is expected. The first-run guide no longer seeds default copies. Add a set from **Widget Library**, or start from a blank canvas in the editor.

### How do I add a Home Screen widget?

1. Save a design in **My Widgets**.
2. Long-press the Home Screen → **＋** → search **iWidget**.
3. Pick a size that matches the design.
4. **Edit Widget** → choose that saved design.

Full steps: **Settings → Help → Add widgets**.

### Does iWidget have extra-large widgets?

Yes, on supported devices: portrait extra-large on iPhone, landscape extra-large on iPad and Mac. The library only lists sizes this device can use.

### Why doesn’t my widget show the design I expect?

- The widget size must match the design (a small board belongs on a small widget).
- After deleting a design, an old widget may still point to it — open the app for the stale-design banner and pick again in **Edit Widget**.
- Adding a new copy never silently replaces a widget already on the Home Screen. Choose the design explicitly.

### What is tap shield?

When **intercept taps** is on, tapping the widget **does not** open the app — useful for wallpaper-style boards. Turn it off in the editor **Behavior** page if you want the tap to open iWidget.

### How do transparent widgets work?

Capture light and dark **Home Screen screenshots**, crop **per slot**, and enable a transparent background. Screenshots affect in-app preview only; the system draws the real wallpaper. See **Add widgets** in Help. iPad can preview landscape and portrait separately.

### Can I add photos and app launchers?

Yes. Photo blocks support crop, rotate, masks, and stickers. Launch blocks can be a single app, a row, a ring, or a free layout. Opening depends on the app, the link, and the system.

### How do I share or import a design?

Export a `.iwidget` file or an offline QR code from **My Widgets**. Import from Files, a scan, or `iwidget://import`. You preview first, then save as a **new** design.

### Does iWidget work offline?

**Yes** for calendar, almanac, editor, and rendering. **Weather** needs a network when refreshing. Holiday adjustments may update when online.

### Do I need an account?

**No.** Optional **iCloud** uses your Apple ID through CloudKit — not an iHTC Studio account.

### What does iCloud sync?

Design files and cropped wallpaper assets in a private CloudKit zone. Off by default. Turning it off stops sync; local files remain until you delete them.

### How do notifications work?

All **local** — up to **48**, looking about three weeks ahead. Calendar types default to 20:00 the evening before; countdowns default to 09:00 on the day. Toggle types in **Settings → Notifications**.

### Which languages are supported?

**Settings → Appearance → Language:** System, 简体中文, 繁體中文, English. The in-app language can differ from the system language.

### How do Siri and Shortcuts work?

Suggested phrases include *“Today’s lunar in iWidget”* and *“Next festival in iWidget”*. Add the **Workday** intent in Shortcuts for a specific date. URLs: [URL Scheme](../url-scheme).

### Does iWidget collect personal data?

No. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy): iHTC Studio does not collect data. Weather, Health, and location are used only when you turn those features on. Health access is read-only.

### Is the app free?

Download is free. The full editor is available for **7 days (168 hours)** from first use, without subscribing and without an automatic charge. Then choose monthly, annual, or lifetime membership. You can still browse the library, add templates, import, export, and use saved designs without a membership. Widgets stay available during the free experience and an active membership; when that access ends they pause until you become a member. Details: [Membership](../membership) · [Terms](../terms)

### Is iWidget on the App Store?

Yes — [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6817595772). Contact [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) for support.

### Still need help?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

### 爱组件是日历 App 吗？

不是。爱组件是 **DIY 小组件设计工坊**。日历、天气、照片和启动入口都是网格上的 **积木**。**今日** 是便捷总览；主屏小组件使用 **我的组件** 里 **已保存的设计**。

### 和爱日历有什么区别？

不同产品、界面与组件风格。爱组件侧重 **排版**、**壁纸调色**、**透明槽位** 与 **误触拦截**。农历算法同源，但 App Store 上是独立应用。

### 现在有多少套设计和积木？

**72** 套设计，**70** 种可添加积木。组件库可按套或按尺寸浏览。支持的设备另有超大尺寸。同一套可存成多份，分别适配不同壁纸。

### 第一次打开后，「我的组件」为什么是空的？

这是预期行为。首次引导不再自动放入默认副本。请从 **组件库** 添加一套，或在编辑器从空白画布开始。

### 如何添加主屏小组件？

1. 在 **我的组件** 保存设计。
2. 长按主屏 → **＋** → 搜索「爱组件」。
3. 选择与设计匹配的尺寸。
4. **编辑小组件** → 选择该设计。

完整步骤：**设置 → 帮助 → 添加小组件**。

### 有超大尺寸吗？

有。支持的设备上，iPhone 可使用竖版超大，iPad 与 Mac 可使用横版超大。组件库只列出本机可用的尺寸。

### 小组件显示不对怎么办？

- 尺寸须与设计一致（小号看板对应小号组件）。
- 删除设计后，旧组件可能仍指向它 — 打开 App 查看失效横幅，并在 **编辑小组件** 里重选。
- 新添加的副本不会悄悄替换已经放在主屏上的组件，需要你明确选择。

### 什么是误触拦截？

设计开启 **拦截跳转** 时，点按小组件 **不会** 打开 App，适合纯壁纸看板。若希望点按打开爱组件，到编辑器 **行为** 页关闭即可。

### 透明小组件怎么用？

截取浅色与深色 **主屏幕截图**，**按槽位裁切**，并启用透明底。截图只影响 App 内预览，真实壁纸由系统绘制。详见帮助中的 **添加小组件**。iPad 可分别预览横屏与竖屏。

### 可以放照片和启动 App 吗？

可以。照片框支持裁切、旋转、遮罩与贴纸。启动积木可以是单个 App、一排、环形或自由排列。能否打开取决于目标 App、链接和系统。

### 如何分享或导入设计？

在 **我的组件** 导出 `.iwidget` 文件或离线二维码。可从文件、扫码或 `iwidget://import` 导入。先预览，再存成 **新的** 设计。

### 能否离线使用？

**农历、黄历、编辑器与渲染** 可离线。**天气** 刷新需要联网。调休安排可能在联网时更新。

### 需要账号吗？

**不需要。** 可选 **iCloud** 走 Apple CloudKit，不是爱火腿肠工作室账号。

### iCloud 同步什么？

设计文件与裁切壁纸（私有 CloudKit 区域）。默认关闭；关闭后停止同步，本机文件仍保留直至删除。

### 通知如何工作？

全部为 **本地通知**，最多 **48** 条，约看未来三周。日历类默认前一天 20:00，倒数日默认当天 09:00。在 **设置 → 通知** 按类型开关。

### 支持哪些语言？

**设置 → 外观 → 语言**：跟随系统、简体中文、繁体中文、English。应用内语言可以和系统语言不同。

### Siri 与快捷指令？

建议短语如「爱组件 今天农历」「爱组件 下一个节日」。**是否上班** 需在快捷指令里单独添加。URL 见 [URL Scheme 说明](../url-scheme)。

### 会收集个人数据吗？

不会。见 [隐私政策](../privacy)：爱火腿肠工作室不收集数据；天气、健康与定位仅在你启用相关功能时使用。健康权限为只读。

### 应用免费吗？

免费下载。首次使用起可连续 **7 天（168 小时）** 体验完整编辑，无需提前订阅，也不会自动扣费。之后可选择月度、年度或永久会员。未购买会员仍可浏览组件库、添加模板、导入导出并使用已保存设计。体验期和会员有效时桌面组件保持显示；到期后会暂停，开通后恢复。详见 [会员说明](../membership) · [使用条款](../terms)

### 是否已上架 App Store？

是的 — [在 App Store 下载](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6817595772)。支持问题可联系 [AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com)。

### 仍需帮助？

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — 通常 48 小时内回复。
