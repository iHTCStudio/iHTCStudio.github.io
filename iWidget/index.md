**Your Home Screen. Your ideas.**

**iWidget** (爱组件 / 愛組件) is a **DIY widget studio** for **iPhone, iPad, and Mac** — not another calendar app. Time, weather, photos, text, countdowns, and app launchers are **blocks** you place on a fine grid. Start from **72 design sets**, or from a blank canvas.

## Start here

1. Finish the **first-run guide**. **My Widgets** stays empty — add what you want from the library.
2. Open **Today** for the solar and lunar date, holidays, almanac, weather, and countdowns.
3. Browse **Widget Library** — by set or by size, with featured and category filters. Search a set name, preview the sizes on this device, then **Add** or **Add & Edit**.
4. In **My Widgets**, search or sort your copies, then tap a card to open the **editor**.
5. On iPhone or iPad: long-press the Home Screen → **＋** → search **iWidget** → pick a size → **Edit Widget** → choose a **saved design**.
6. **Settings → Help** has four full-screen guides: user guide, add widgets, Shortcuts & Siri, and highlights.

## Membership

Download is free. You can use the full editor for **7 days (168 hours)** from first use on this device, without subscribing and without an automatic charge. After that, choose monthly, annual, or lifetime membership — every plan has the same editing tools.

You can still browse the library, add templates, import, export, and use saved designs without a membership. Widgets stay available during the free experience and an active membership; when that access ends they pause, then resume after you become a member. Editing requires an active membership or the free experience. Prices come from Apple. Details: [Membership](membership) · [Terms of Use](terms)

## Library & My Widgets

| Area | What you get |
|------|----------------|
| **Library** | **72** design sets. Browse **by set** or **by size**. Featured and category filters; search set or member names. Highlight chips jump to the relevant block. Extra-large sizes appear on supported devices. |
| **My Widgets** | Saved copies, grouped by size; light/dark preview; search; sort by manual order, name, newest, oldest, or **template**. The same set can exist as multiple copies. |
| **Keep & share** | Rename, duplicate, delete. Export a `.iwidget` file or an offline QR code; import from Files, a scan, or `iwidget://import`. Preview before saving as a new design. |

The library keeps the original. Your edits live in **My Widgets**, so trying a new wallpaper does not overwrite the template.

## Editor

- **Fine grid** — drag, resize, and let neighbors make room. Each size can keep its own layout. Undo 30 steps.
- **Multi-select** — align, copy appearance, unify corners and borders, or work with layers. Overlap is optional; hidden blocks stay in the editor and leave the Home Screen.
- **Appearance** — type, weight, gradient ink, panels, borders, shadows, and per-block fonts. Copy appearance or data options to other sizes. **16** gradient presets; light and dark can use different colors.
- **Wallpaper** — solid, gradient, or transparent. Import a Home Screen screenshot to preview slot alignment. Screenshots affect in-app preview only; real transparency depends on the system.
- **Behavior** — week start and temperature unit per design; **tap shield** so glances stay on the Home Screen; optional Apple Weather credit placement.

On supported devices, **Describe colors** (Apple Intelligence) can suggest a palette.

## Blocks

**70** addable types. Library previews may show sample data; Home Screen widgets use live data.

| Family | Examples |
|--------|----------|
| **Date** | Day number, date line, weekday, work/rest, month grid, week strip, year progress |
| **Lunar** | Lunar date, stems-branches, festivals, solar terms, almanac, phenology, moon phase |
| **Time** | Digital clock, analog face, day progress |
| **Weather** | Symbol, temperature, condition, high/low, place, hourly, daily, sunrise/sunset — Apple Weather |
| **Countdown** | Title and days; solar or lunar; repeat or once |
| **Photo** | Crop, rotate, masks, stickers, optional overflow |
| **Launch** | Single app, row, ring, or free layout |
| **Device** | Battery, storage, memory, processor, thermal, network, connectivity, music, device info, steps |
| **Decor** | Text, greeting, divider, symbol, spinner |

Weather symbols, temperature, and condition can sit in separate blocks. Several symbol styles are available. Launch blocks open the chosen app when the system allows; you can also leave a few shortcuts next to weather or a photo.

## Today

A quiet dashboard with a consistent card style:

- **Solar & lunar** — Gregorian line, lunar date, stems-branches, zodiac, work/rest badges.
- **Solar term** — interval progress, phenology, a short poem; tap through to the day.
- **Weather** — shown when enabled in **Settings → Weather display**; pull to refresh. The hourly row can scroll history and forecast.
- **Upcoming** — up to 8 items; statutory rest is grouped, makeup workdays appear separately. **See all** covers about a year.
- **Countdowns** — preview up to 5; **All** opens the list with manual / date / name sort.
- **Almanac** — daily tags and clash notes; tap for day detail.

Tap the date for the month grid, then day detail. You can hide or reorder cards after tapping **Edit** on Today.

## Notifications (optional)

Local notifications only — up to **48**, looking about three weeks ahead:

- Calendar types default to **20:00** the evening before (you can switch to the same day).
- Countdowns default to **09:00** on the day (1 / 3 / 7 days earlier is optional).

Toggle each type in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Settings

- **Appearance** — app light/dark/system; **in-app language** (System / 简体 / 繁體 / English); festival layers; **location or a manual city**; weather display; wallpaper; Health for steps (iOS). Week start lives in the month calendar and can be overridden per design.
- **Access** — location, Bluetooth, Apple Music, Health, and notifications, with a jump to system settings when needed.
- **iCloud** — off by default; when on, syncs designs and cropped wallpapers through CloudKit on your Apple ID.
- **Data** — design and countdown counts; import a `.iwidget` file or QR code.
- **About** — rate, optional tip (unlocks nothing), feedback email with diagnostics, more apps, licenses.

## Siri, Shortcuts & URLs

- Suggested phrases include *“Today’s lunar in iWidget”* and *“Next festival in iWidget”*.
- A separate **Workday** App Intent asks whether a date is a work or rest day (add it in Shortcuts).
- Deep links: `iwidget://today`, `iwidget://library`, `iwidget://mine`, `iwidget://editor`, `iwidget://import`, `iwidget://membership` — see [URL Scheme](url-scheme).

## Data & privacy

- Lunar dates, festivals, solar terms, and the almanac compute **on device**.
- Weather uses **Apple Weather** when online, and can use a manual city so location is optional.
- Health access is optional and **read-only** (steps and walking distance).
- **No iHTC Studio account.** Optional iCloud is Apple-hosted. Details: [Privacy Policy](privacy).

**你的桌面，不必和别人长得一样。**

**爱组件**（iWidget / 愛組件）是面向 **iPhone、iPad 与 Mac** 的 **DIY 小组件设计工坊**——不是换皮日历。时钟、日期、天气、照片、文字、倒数和启动入口都是可摆放的 **积木**。从 **72 套设计** 起步，也可以从空白画布一块一块拼。

## 快速上手

1. 完成 **首次引导**。**我的组件** 会保持为空，请从组件库自行添加。
2. 打开 **今日**，查看公历农历、节日、黄历、天气与倒数。
3. 浏览 **组件库** — 可按套或按尺寸，配合精选与分类；搜索套名，预览本机尺寸后 **添加** 或 **添加并编辑**。
4. 在 **我的组件** 搜索或排序副本，点卡片进入 **编辑器**。
5. iPhone / iPad：长按主屏 → **＋** → 搜索「爱组件」→ 选尺寸 → **编辑小组件** → 选择 **已保存设计**。
6. **设置 → 帮助** 有四本全屏教程：使用说明、添加小组件、快捷指令与 Siri、功能亮点。

未购买会员仍可浏览组件库、添加模板、导入导出，并使用已保存设计。体验期和会员有效时，桌面组件保持显示；到期后会暂停，开通后恢复。进入编辑器需要有效会员或免费体验。价格由 Apple 提供。详见 [会员说明](membership) · [使用条款](terms)

## 编辑器

- **细网格** — 拖动、缩放，邻块会让位。同一设计的不同尺寸可各自排法。撤销 30 步。
- **多选** — 对齐、复制外观、统一圆角与边框，或使用图层。重叠可选；隐藏的积木只留在编辑器，桌面不绘制。
- **外观** — 字体、字重、渐变文字、底框、边框、阴影，以及单块换字体。外观或数据选项可复制到其他尺寸。**16** 套渐变预设；浅色与深色可分别取色。
- **壁纸** — 纯色、渐变或透明。可导入主屏幕截图，预览槽位对齐。截图只影响 App 内预览；透明效果取决于系统。
- **行为** — 可为设计单独设置周起始与温标；**误触拦截** 让轻触留在主屏；可调整 Apple Weather 署名位置。

支持的设备上，可用 **按描述配色**（Apple Intelligence）生成配色建议。

## 今日

风格统一的概览页：

- **公历与农历** — 日期行、农历、干支生肖、休班角标。
- **节气** — 区间进度、物候、短诗；可进入日详情。
- **天气** — 由 **设置 → 天气展示** 控制；支持下拉刷新。逐时条可滚动历史与预报。
- **即将到来** — 最多 8 条；法定放假按连续休息归并，调休补班单独列出。**查看全部** 约覆盖一年。
- **倒数日** — 预览最多 5 条；**全部** 支持手动 / 日期 / 名称排序。
- **黄历** — 宜忌与冲煞；点进当日详情。

点日期打开月历，再进日详情。今日右上角 **编辑** 后，可排序或隐藏卡片。

## 通知（可选）

仅 **本地通知**，最多 **48** 条，约看未来三周：

- 日历类默认前一天 **20:00**（可改为当天）。
- 倒数日默认当天 **09:00**（也可提前 1 / 3 / 7 天）。

在 **设置 → 通知** 按类型开关。

## 设置

- **外观** — App 深浅色；**应用内语言**（跟随系统 / 简体 / 繁体 / English）；节日分层；**定位或手动城市**；天气展示；壁纸；Health 步数（iOS）。周起始在月历中设置，也可按设计单独指定。
- **授权** — 定位、蓝牙、Apple Music、健康与通知，需要时可跳转系统设置。
- **iCloud** — 默认关闭；开启后通过 CloudKit 同步设计与裁切壁纸。
- **数据** — 本机设计数与倒数条目；可导入 `.iwidget` 或二维码。
- **关于** — 评分、可选赞赏（不解锁功能）、带诊断的反馈邮件、更多 App、许可说明。
