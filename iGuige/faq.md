<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Guigu Academy, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

It is a **Guiguzi workshop**: 40 art cards with misuse warnings, 200 Warring States cases, 100 modern study scenes, and Speak to Each Kind — a method desk, not a sales script farm.

### What texts are included?

21 extant chapters (381 sentences) plus 2 lost titles as placeholders; 40 art cards; 200 cases; 100 scenes; 30 figures; a timeline; 220 famous lines; 64 concepts. Originals are public-domain. Art cards, debriefs, and Speak to Each Kind scripts are study aids.

### Is Speak to Each Kind a consulting product?

**No.** It is a study drill that walks back to *Assessing Words*. It does not hire out speeches or close deals for you.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

The listing is being prepared. Bookmark this support page; the [App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813769347) will open once Apple publishes the app.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.

这是**鬼谷子研习工坊**：40 张带误用警示的术卡、200 则战国案例、100 例现代学习场景，以及与谁言——方法书桌，不是销售话术工厂。
