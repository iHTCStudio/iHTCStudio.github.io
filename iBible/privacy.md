iHTC Studio built iBible (爱圣经 / 愛聖經).

## Summary

**Data Not Collected.** iBible is designed so that **core Scripture reading works without sending your data to iHTC Studio**. **No account. No ads. No analytics SDK.** Bible text is **bundled on your device**. Optional **iCloud Sync** for favorites and verse lists uses **your private iCloud** only.

## Data Stored on Your Device

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Bible database | Bundled Scripture — 66 books, ~31,021 verses |
| Favorites & verse lists | Verses and lists you create |
| Highlights & reading state | Colors, last-read chapter, streak, plan progress |
| Preferences | Theme, accents, TTS, bilingual layout, notifications |
| Notification settings | Today’s Verse reminders (local scheduling only) |

## Network

After install, **everyday reading does not require the internet**. Opening App Store pages, sending email feedback, or completing an optional tip uses **Apple** or **your mail app** — not an iHTC Studio backend for Scripture.

## iCloud Sync

Optional sync for **favorites** and **verse lists** through your private iCloud key-value store. Scripture content itself is not uploaded to iHTC Studio servers.

## Permissions

| Permission | When | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| Notifications | Today’s Verse reminder | Local scheduling only — no server |
| iCloud | Optional sync | Sync favorites and verse lists |
| Photos (add) | Save share cards | On-device images you choose to save |
