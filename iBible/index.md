**Let God’s Word light your every day.**

iBible (爱圣经) is a **Chinese–English bilingual Bible reading & devotion studio** for iPhone, iPad and Mac — not another verse list, not a social network, not a commentary paywall. Open to **Today’s Verse**, read in **parallel Chinese and English**, walk a **reading plan**, mark what stirs you, and listen with background TTS. **66 books**, **~31,021 verses** — the full Bible on your device. **No account. No ads. No tracking. Fully offline.**

**At a glance:** Today’s Verse · bilingual parallel reading · 22 topics · 80 famous verses · 6 reading plans · highlights & verse lists · share cards · TTS with Now Playing · 5 widgets · Live Activity · Siri & `ibible://` · optional iCloud for favorites & lists.

> Rediscover the beauty of Scripture — verse by verse, day by day.

## Getting Started

1. Complete the short **welcome guide** on first launch (replay anytime in **Settings → Guides**).
2. Start from **Today’s Verse** — a verse chosen for the day (weekday + liturgical season, including a dynamic Easter window).
3. Open any chapter for **immersive reading** — primary + parallel translation, verse numbers, paragraph mode, tap the title to pick book & chapter.
4. Turn on **Read Aloud** — adjust rate, pitch and voice; keep listening with **background audio** and lock-screen **Now Playing**.
5. Explore **Topics**, **Famous Verses**, and **Reading Plans** (7 days to a full-year through-Bible).
6. **Highlight** in 5 colors, star favorites, build custom **verse lists**, and track your reading streak.
7. Add **widgets** (Today’s Verse, Famous, Favorites, Plan Progress, Continue Reading) — including Lock Screen styles.
8. Ask **Siri** for today’s verse, a random verse, or to start reading aloud. Use `ibible://` from Shortcuts.

## Why you’ll want to download

- **A verse that finds you** — Today’s Verse syncs across App, widget, notification and deep link, so the same light meets you on the Lock Screen and at your desk.
- **Bilingual by design** — UI language and Scripture language are independent. Flip Chinese ↔ English side by side; swap which version is primary.
- **Privacy you can feel** — the whole Bible is bundled. After install, core reading never needs the network. No account to “unlock” Scripture.
- **Apple-native depth** — widgets you can refresh, Live Activity while reading, Control Center (iOS 18+), Siri, Spotlight, alternate icons, parchment UI with 9 accent themes.
- **Devotion without noise** — plans, topics and famous verses curated for daily life — love, peace, anxiety, prayer, hope — without feeds, streaks-for-show or ads.

## はじめに

1. 最初の起動時に短い **ウェルカム ガイド**を完了してください (**設定 → ガイド**でいつでも再生できます)。
2. **今日の聖句** — その日のために選ばれた聖句 (平日 + 典礼シーズン、ダイナミックなイースター期間を含む) から始めます。
3. **没入型読書**のために任意の章を開いてください。初版 + 対訳、節番号、段落モード、タイトルをタップして本と章を選択します。
4. **読み上げ** をオンにする — 速度、ピッチ、音声を調整します。 **バックグラウンドオーディオ**とロック画面**再生中**で聞き続けてください。
5. **トピック**、**有名な聖句**、**読書計画**を検討してください (聖書を通読する 7 日間から 1 年間)。
6. 5 色で **ハイライト**、お気に入りにスターを付け、カスタム **詩リスト**を作成し、読書の記録を追跡します。
7. **ウィジェット** (今日の詩、有名な曲、お気に入り、計画の進捗状況、続きを読む) を追加します。ロック画面のスタイルも含まれます。
8. Ask **Siri** for today’s verse, a random verse, or to start reading aloud. Use `ibible://` from Shortcuts.

## Особенности

### Сегодняшний стих

- Контекстный выбор по **дню недели** и **сезону** (Адвент, Рождество, Пасхальное окно, День Благодарения, Новый год и т. д.)

### Двуязычное рабочее место для чтения
