<section lang="en" markdown="1">

### Is the app fully offline?

Yes for core use. The library and study aids ship on device. After install, browsing, close reading, recite (system TTS), Reform Study, widgets, and local daily notifications work **without network**. Optional exceptions: **StoreKit tipping** (Apple), sharing to another app you choose, and optional iCloud.

### How is this different from a simple ebook?

It is a **Legalist history desk**: four-layer reading (including Duyvendak 1928), about 60 reform edicts, a 361–338 BCE chronicle, and a fourteen-person graph — study, not a slogan for how to rule today.

### What texts are included?

24 extant chapters, 2 lost titles, a Six Laws fragment; 1,222 sentences aligned four ways; 204 famous lines; 60 edicts; 50 concepts; 30 chronicle events; 14 figures. Original and 1928 English are public-domain. Vernacular and modern study English are study aids.

### Does “How to use” tell me how to govern today?

No. It is a self-check reading aid (wrong / right / next step) for understanding institutional design. Sensitive measures carry an in-app caution.

### What does iCloud Sync cover?

Optional sync for **favorites**, **lists**, **notes**, **reading history**, **study progress**, and **streaks** via your private iCloud KVS.

### Do you collect my data?

**No.** See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy): Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio; no ads/analytics SDK; no account.

### Where is the privacy policy URL for App Store Connect?

Open this site's [Privacy Policy](../privacy) page and copy the URL from your browser's address bar.

### Is it on the App Store yet?

The listing is being prepared. Bookmark this support page; the [App Store link](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6813769520) will open once Apple publishes the app.

### System requirements?

iPhone / iPad **iOS 17.0+**, Mac **macOS 14.0+**.

### How do I contact support?

[AppleOSer@gmail.com](mailto:AppleOSer@gmail.com) — typically within 48 hours.
