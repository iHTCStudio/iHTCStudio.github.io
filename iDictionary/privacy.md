<section lang="en" markdown="1">

iHTC Studio built iDictionary (爱字词典 / 愛字詞典). This page describes our data practices.

## Summary

**iDictionary does not require an account and does not collect personal data on iHTC Studio servers.** All dictionary content is stored on your device after first-launch setup. Optional **iCloud Sync** can keep favorites and preferences in step across your devices through **your private iCloud** — default **off**. **No ads. No tracking.**

## Data Collection

- **No account** — no registration, email, or phone number required.
- **No analytics or ads** — no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- **No cloud dictionary** — lookups never leave your device (except optional iCloud for your own favorites).

## Data Stored on Your Device

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Inflated dictionary database | Offline hanzi, words, idioms, stroke SVG |
| Favorites | Characters, words, idioms you star |
| Browse history | Recent lookups |
| Preferences | Theme, language, TTS settings |
| Onboarding state | Whether you've seen guides |
| iCloud sync toggle | Local preference for enabling sync |

## iCloud Sync

Optional iCloud sync keeps favorites and preferences in step across devices signed in to the same Apple ID. Data syncs through **your private iCloud key-value store** — not iHTC Studio servers. **Disabled by default.**

## Permissions

| Permission | When | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| Camera | OCR lookup | Recognize characters in photos — processed on-device |
| Microphone | Not used | — |
| iCloud | Optional sync | Sync your favorites across devices |

## Children's Privacy

iDictionary does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

## Contact

| Entpackte Wörterbuchdatenbank | Offline-Zeichen, -Wörter, -Idiome und Strichfolge-SVG |
| Favoriten | Von Ihnen mit einem Stern markierte Zeichen, Wörter und Idiome |
| Verlauf | Letzte Suchanfragen |
| Einstellungen | Design, Sprache, TTS-Einstellungen |
| Onboarding-Status | Ob Sie die Anleitungen schon gesehen haben |
| iCloud-Synchronisierungsschalter | Lokale Einstellung zum Aktivieren der Synchronisierung |

## iCloud-Synchronisierung

Die optionale iCloud-Synchronisierung hält Favoriten und Einstellungen auf Geräten abgeglichen, die mit derselben Apple-ID angemeldet sind. Die Daten werden über **Ihren privaten iCloud-Schlüssel-Wert-Speicher** synchronisiert — nicht über Server von iHTC Studio. **Standardmäßig deaktiviert.**

| Mikrofon | Nicht verwendet | — |
| iCloud | Optionale Synchronisierung | Favoriten geräteübergreifend synchronisieren |
