iHTC Studio built iZen (爱禅 / 愛禪).

## Summary

**Data Not Collected by iHTC Studio.** Core recovery features work **without sending your data to iHTC Studio**. **No account. No ads. No analytics SDK.** Practices, quotes, science, and crisis helpline data are **bundled on your device**. Optional **iCloud Sync** uses **your private iCloud** only.

## Data Stored on Your Device

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Content library | Bundled practices, quotes, science, crisis helplines |
| Favorites & Zen lists | Starred items and custom collections |
| Session history | Breathing / tool session records |
| Journal, assessments, three tasks, cognitive entries | Your structured logs |
| Streaks & stats | Recovery dashboard and pressure curve |
| Preferences | Theme, accent, breathing defaults, notifications, Live Activity, Spotlight |

## Network

After install, **everyday use does not require the internet**. Opening App Store pages, crisis **web** links, sending email feedback, or completing an optional tip uses **Apple**, **your browser**, or **your mail app** — not an iHTC Studio backend for content.

Crisis phone/SMS actions use the **system dialer / Messages** when you tap a link.

## iCloud Sync (optional)

When enabled and signed into iCloud, favorites, Zen lists, logs, assessments, streaks, and related progress may sync via your private iCloud key-value store. iHTC Studio cannot read your iCloud container.

## Permissions

| Permission | When | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| Notifications | Daily Zen / assessment reminders | Local scheduling only |
| iCloud | Optional sync | Mirror your progress |
| Health (Mindfulness) | Optional, off by default | Write minutes you choose |
| Photos (add) | Save share cards | On-device images you choose |
| Spotlight | Optional, off by default | Index content on device |

## Medical disclaimer

iZen offers wellness and educational content plus a bundled crisis directory. It is **not** a medical device, therapist, or emergency service. Seek professional care or local emergency services when needed.
