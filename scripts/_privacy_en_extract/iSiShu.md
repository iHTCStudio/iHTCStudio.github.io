iHTC Studio built **iSiShu** (爱四书 / 愛四書, Bundle ID `com.iHTCboy.iSiShu`). We do **not** operate a backend that collects your personal data.

## Summary

- **Account:** not required
- **Data collection by us:** **Data Not Collected**
- **Core use:** offline after install
- **Ads / analytics / tracking:** none
- **Optional Apple services:** iCloud KVS, Photo Library add-only, StoreKit tipping, local notifications, Spotlight, Live Activity

## Stored locally

- Favorites and lists
- Reading history and notes
- Study progress, mastery, and streaks
- Appearance, reading, and speech preferences
- Daily Saying cache and widget state

These stay on your device and optionally in **your private iCloud** if you enable sync.

## How the app works

- Text, vernacular, and English are bundled with the app (curated from public web sources).
- Recitation uses Apple system TTS.
- Widgets share state through App Group `group.com.iHTCboy.iSiShu`.
- Notifications are local.
- Share cards are rendered on device.

## Permissions

| Permission | When | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| Notifications | You enable reminders | Local Daily Saying reminders |
| Photo Library (Add Only) | You save a share card | Save rendered images |
| iCloud | You enable sync | Sync favorites, lists, progress, notes |
| Live Activities | You enable reading activity | Lock Screen / Dynamic Island |
