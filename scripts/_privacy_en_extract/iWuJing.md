**Last updated:** August 19, 2026

iHTC Studio built **iWuJing** (爱五经 / 愛五經, Bundle ID `com.iHTCboy.iWuJing`). This app works as an **offline study desk** for the Confucian Five Classics. We do **not** run a backend that collects your personal data.

## Summary

- **Account:** not required
- **Data collection by us:** **Data Not Collected**
- **Core use:** offline after install
- **Ads / analytics / tracking:** none
- **Optional Apple services:** iCloud KVS, Photo Library add-only, StoreKit tipping, local notifications, Spotlight, Live Activity

## What stays on your device

- Favorites and lists
- Reading history and last read position
- Study progress, mastery, streaks, and notes
- Appearance, reading, and speech preferences
- Daily Classic cache and widget state

These stay on your device and optionally in **your private iCloud** if you enable sync.

## How the app works

- Text library, vernacular, English, gloss, and variants are bundled with the app (curated from public web sources).
- Recitation uses Apple system TTS.
- Widgets share lightweight state through App Group `group.com.iHTCboy.iWuJing`.
- Notifications are scheduled locally.
- Share cards are rendered on device.
- Zhouyi content is presented for **classical study only**, not divination.

## Permissions

| Permission | When | Why |
|------------|------|-----|
| Notifications | You enable reminders | Local Daily Classic reminders |
| Photo Library (Add Only) | You save a classic card | Save rendered images |
| iCloud | You enable sync | Sync favorites, lists, progress, notes |
| Live Activities | You enable reading activity | Lock Screen / Dynamic Island |

We do **not** request tracking, contacts, or location.
